6-Step Learning Roadmap

Core Topics You Should Master

  • Clinical workflows and medical terminology
  • Data quality, interoperability (FHIR/HL7), and annotation
  • Model development for tabular, text, and imaging data
  • Evaluation beyond accuracy: sensitivity, specificity, calibration
  • Bias, fairness, and responsible AI in healthcare
  • Regulatory pathways and post-deployment monitoring
